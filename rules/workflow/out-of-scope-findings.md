# Out-of-scope Findings

A problem you notice but are not fixing in this session goes in `{{findingsFile}}`.
Do not fix it, and do not drop it on the floor.

This covers anything outside the branch's scope, anything in a file someone else
is working in, and anything project-wide rather than feature-specific.

Keep each entry to what is wrong, where, and how to fix it. No audit tables, no
verification scripts. Mention it in your reply too, so deferring is the user's
decision rather than a silent one.
